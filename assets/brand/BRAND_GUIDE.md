# FORGE — Geometric identity

Status: selected master direction
Concept: Context Links

## Rationale

Two opposing geometric brackets interlock to form the FORGE monogram. The
construction represents code, linked context and the connection between durable
project memory and semantic retrieval.

## Assets

- `forge-mark-color.svg` — primary two-colour symbol for dark backgrounds.
- `forge-mark-mono-light.svg` — light monochrome symbol for dark backgrounds.
- `forge-mark-mono-dark.svg` — dark monochrome symbol for light backgrounds.
- `forge-lockup-horizontal.svg` — primary horizontal logo and outlined wordmark.
- `forge-favicon.svg` — compact mark on a charcoal rounded-square field.

All files are self-contained SVGs. The wordmark uses paths and has no external
font dependency.

## Colours

- Charcoal — `#0A0B0D`
- Amber — `#F0A83A`
- Bone — `#F2F0EA`
- Steel — `#9299A4` (supporting UI colour; not required in the master logo)

## Usage

- Use the colour mark and horizontal lockup on charcoal or near-black fields.
- Use the light monochrome mark when only one ink or colour is available on dark.
- Use the dark monochrome mark on white, bone or other light backgrounds.
- Use the favicon asset for square app, browser and repository avatars.
- Preserve clear space equal to at least one quarter of the mark width.
- Minimum recommended size: 20 px for the symbol, 120 px for the lockup.

## Avoid

- Do not add gradients, shadows, glow, bevels or outlines.
- Do not rotate, skew, stretch or rearrange the two linked forms.
- Do not recolour individual pieces outside the approved palette.
- Do not place the colour version on amber, bone or visually noisy backgrounds.
